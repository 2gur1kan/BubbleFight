# 🫧 BubbleFight

> A small arena game inspired by the classic Beyblade concept.

BubbleFight is a small Unity game project inspired by the idea of controlling a spinning object in an arena and watching the outcome of a clash.

The project was created as a compact game-development experiment, focusing on simple gameplay, physics-based interactions, and the excitement of letting the player's choices determine the result.

---

## 🎮 About the Game

BubbleFight takes inspiration from the classic Beyblade-style arena battles.

Instead of directly controlling every movement during the fight, the player makes their choice and watches the result unfold.

The core idea is simple:

> **Make your choice. Start the fight. Watch what happens.**

The project was designed as a small-scale gameplay experiment rather than a large production.

Try it : https://systembug.itch.io/bubble-fight

The gameplay revolves around making a choice before the battle begins.

Once the fight starts, the objects interact inside the arena and the physics of the game determine the outcome.
